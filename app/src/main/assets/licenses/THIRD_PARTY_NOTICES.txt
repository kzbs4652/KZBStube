MP3 encoding
-----------
This application bundles net.qiujuer.lame:lame, an Android LAME encoder wrapper.
The wrapper project is available at https://github.com/qiujuer/LameAndroid.
The bundled LAME encoder is licensed under LGPL-2.1. A copy of the license is
provided in this directory as LGPL-2.1.txt. The corresponding source is available
from the upstream project linked above.
